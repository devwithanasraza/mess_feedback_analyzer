Key Principles

🔒

Anonymous Feedback

Your identity stays private while your feedback helps improve the mess. No roll numbers, email addresses, or student IDs are ever recorded.

Feedback Lifecycle

How It Works

A transparent four-step loop that transforms individual dining experiences into institutional improvements.

1

Eat Your Meal

Enjoy breakfast, lunch, snacks, or dinner at your hostel dining hall.

2

Share Honest Opinion

Take 45 seconds to rate dishes and note any food left over without sharing your identity.

3

System Analyzes Feedback

The application classifies sentiments, computes weighted satisfaction, and flags waste drivers.

4

Mess Improves Future Meals

Mess supervisors use automated analytics and reports to optimize recipes and procurement.
